# Privacy Policy — Reels Control Bar

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Reels Control Bar ("the extension") adds a control bar, keyboard shortcuts, playback speed control and auto-next to Reels videos on instagram.com.

## Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, record, store on any server, sell, share or transmit personal data or browsing activity of any kind.

## What the extension accesses

- **Only instagram.com.** The extension runs only on `https://www.instagram.com/*`.
- **Video playback state.** To draw the control bar it reads the state of the video you are watching (current time, duration, paused, muted) and sets the playback position and speed. This happens entirely in your browser and is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Instagram's audio button.** To mute or unmute, the extension clicks Instagram's own audio button on the page.
- **Nothing else on the page.** It does not read captions, comments, messages, usernames, account details or any other page content.

## What the extension stores

Three settings are saved in your browser through Chrome's `storage` API:

- whether the extension is enabled,
- whether auto-next is on,
- your chosen playback speed.

If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome may sync these settings between your own devices through your Google account. We have no access to them.

## Network requests and third parties

The extension makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and it does not load or run code from remote sources.

## Children

The extension does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If the extension's behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed.
